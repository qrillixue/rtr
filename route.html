From heritage alleys to riverside trails

A seamless transition from century-old colonial architecture to untouched river wilderness.

Stage 1

Ipoh Old Town Heritage

Glide silently through historic alleys and past famous street art murals without disturbing the old town's tranquility.

  • British colonial architecture backdrop
  • Zero exhaust, peaceful coexistence with pedestrians
  • Time to get comfortable with the throttle

Terrain: smooth pavers and tarmac

Stage 2

Kinta River Trails

As the asphalt fades, follow loose riverbank gravel, dirt tracks and bamboo groves running alongside the historic Kinta waterway.

  • Instant electric power over riverbank gravel
  • Natural water splashes and berm turns
  • Suspension that soaks up the bumps

Terrain: dirt, river pebble and berms

Stage 3

Kledang Mountain Vista

Arrive at an open plain under the Kledang Range for a golden-hour breather, portrait photos and a cold drink.

  • Mountain sunset horizon backdrop
  • Dedicated photo stop
  • Refreshment break

Terrain: open plain and riverbank
