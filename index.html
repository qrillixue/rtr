The story behind RTR

RTR River Trail Ride is a guided e-bike experience based in Ipoh, Perak, built around the river that runs through the city.

Most visitors see Ipoh from a car window or a checklist of the usual stops. RTR follows the river instead, through quiet enclaves and beautiful lanes, to hidden sights and the stories behind them.

Every ride is limited to 2 guests and led by a certified guide. The e-bikes take the sting out of the heat and the inclines, and the helmet intercom keeps the stories going while you ride.

What's included, and what to know

Rider requirements

No motorcycle license needed, since the e-bikes are fully automatic twist and go. Closed-toe shoes are mandatory, so no flip-flops.

What if it rains?

Light tropical drizzle makes the river trail even more fun. If there is extreme lightning or a flash flood risk, we offer free rescheduling or a full refund.

Do I need riding experience?

No. The e-bikes are twist and go with no clutch to stall, so beginners can ride comfortably. Your guide gets everyone set up before the ride begins.

How many people are on a ride?

A maximum of 2 guests per ride, led by one guide. It is never a crowded tour group, and you can ask questions at any time.

How does the helmet intercom work?

Each helmet has a built-in intercom that connects you to your guide and your riding partner. There is no push-to-talk. Talk as you would on a phone call, and keep both hands on the bars.

Can I adjust the volume or mute it?

Yes. The volume can be adjusted and the channel can be muted if you would rather ride in quiet.

What should I wear?

Closed-toe shoes are mandatory, so no flip-flops. Helmet and protective gear are provided.

Will I get photos of my ride?

Your guide can also be your photographer along the route.

Ready to ride the river?

Send us your date and group size and we will try to get back to you as soon as possible.
